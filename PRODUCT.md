## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

豆哥在此 is a personal article site. Readers browse articles, find related topics, and read Chinese and English text, including technical examples.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site uses Jekyll and the Chirpy theme, configured in `_config.yml` and `Gemfile`. Article content remains in `_posts/`. Search, categories, tags, archives, and article navigation retain their existing routes.

Reading controls open and close the navigation and article panels, show an article contents dialog, and change the reading font. Keyboard shortcuts belong to `assets/js/reading-mode.js`.

Paper and Cursor coexist as selectable appearances. Light, dark, and system color modes remain independent of appearance. Each appearance retains its own reading-font preference.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the site name, avatar, article text, and existing navigation. Preserve Paper's visual identity. Cursor's website and blog are the approved reference for the additional appearance: sans-serif typography, restrained surfaces, and fine boundaries. Both appearances share Paper's responsive article width and side gutters.

## Evidence on Hand

`_config.yml` contains site identity and settings. `_includes/` and `_layouts/default.html` contain the shared page structure. Real articles in `_posts/` provide the content for desktop and mobile verification.
